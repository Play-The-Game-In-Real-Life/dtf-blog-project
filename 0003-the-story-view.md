*Recording begins. The defendant's phone is on the table, screen down. It has been face down since he sat.*

**Det. Holloway:** Did you look at the story?

**Joe:** No.

**Det. Holloway:** The platform says you did.

**Joe:** I tapped. I didn't *look*. I tapped so fast I didn't absorb any of it.

**Det. Holloway:** What did it show?

**Joe:** A sandwich.

*Pause.*

**Joe:** A really nice sandwich.

**Det. Holloway:** You absorbed the sandwich.

**Joe:** I absorbed the *outline* of the sandwich.

> **Exhibit C:** Treaty signed by the defendant, dated 2026-10-14. Article I: "The Signatory agrees not to look." Article II: "The Signatory acknowledges he is going to look."

**Det. Holloway:** You wrote Article II yourself.

**Joe:** It was a very honest treaty.

---

**Findings of the court:** The defendant looked. The defendant has also, to be fair, never claimed to be a man of his word, only a man of excellent documentation.

**Sentence:** Touch grass. This is not a request.
