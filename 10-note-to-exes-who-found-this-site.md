Hi. You found it. I knew this would happen, and the analytics page confirms that some of you have already been through twice.

## First, the clarifications

- **Your name is not here.** Every realm has a codename. If you think you recognize yourself, I would encourage you to be flattered.
- **Everything nice I said was true.** That includes the couch, the drawer, and the Tuesday.
- **Everything I said about myself was also true.** Possibly generous.

## Second, the apology

I am sorry for the things I did and the things I did not do, especially the second kind. The site is a comedy, but the history underneath it was real, and I know that it was lived by someone other than me, who was probably very tired of waiting for a reply.

## Third, a request

If you would like to be removed, edited, renamed, or turned into a more flattering color, tell me and it will be done. I mean this. A good joke is not worth a bad afternoon for someone who did not sign up to be in it.

## Fourth, a note about the analytics

Yes, the site runs analytics. No, I am not going to go through the logs looking for you. I did that once, for about four minutes, and learned that I am exactly the kind of person who would.

I hope you are well. I hope it is going great. Thank you for reading this far.

*The Capital remains under renovation. Visitors are welcome and will be gently ignored.*
