Every archive has a section nobody wants to open, and this is mine. The Stonelands predate the First Age, the lore, and my ability to explain what I was doing.

## The records

Very little survives. There is a handful of photos, a playlist with a name I would not say out loud, and a memory of a conversation I have replayed so many times it has probably become fiction. A good historian would flag that. I am a bad historian with a strong sense of how I came off.

## The age of stone

Things were simpler then, in the sense that I had no vocabulary for what I was feeling and used a different, worse method: acting like nothing was happening, and being extremely intense about it. Nothing was carved with care. Everything was carved with confidence.

## The fall

It ended before the maps were drawn, which is convenient, since I cannot be held to any particular version of events. I will say it ended the way early things do. Someone grew, and it was not both of us at the same pace.

## What survives

The main lesson of the Stonelands is humbling. I was not the protagonist I thought I was. I was a supporting character in someone else's growth, and I responded by building a personality around being misunderstood.

The ruins remain, and I am told they are tasteful.

*Status: ancient history. If this chronicle seems light on detail, it is because the detail is where I come off worst.*
