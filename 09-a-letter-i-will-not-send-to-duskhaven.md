I keep writing this one and not sending it, which is something I have gotten very good at.

The version I will publish here has no names and no specifics, because it is not for anyone else to read. It is just a place to put it.

## The part that I mean

I am proud of what we built. I want to say that without any of the usual jokes around it. For a while, we made a place where two people were safe, and that is rarer than I understood at the time.

I am sorry for the years I spent mistaking attention for care. You deserved someone who did the upkeep, and I was good at everything except the thing that mattered.

I hope the couch went to a good home. I hope you have a drawer somewhere that is entirely yours.

## The part that is also true

I do not want to go back. I want you to know that, because it would be easy for this to sound like something else. What I want is to have been better, and that is not available, and I am learning to let it be a thing I carry instead of a thing I fix.

## The signoff

Thank you for the first kingdom. It was a good one.

I am going to leave this in drafts, and I think that is the right place for it.
