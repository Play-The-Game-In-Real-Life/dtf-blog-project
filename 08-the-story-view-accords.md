**Article I.** The Signatory agrees not to look at the stories of any former realm.

**Article II.** The Signatory acknowledges that he is going to look at one of them. It is not clear which. It will be the one he is least ready for.

## Enforcement

The Accords are enforced by the Signatory's own willpower, which has historically performed about as well as the Treaty of Versailles, though with a slightly better view of what everyone ate for lunch.

## The loophole

The Signatory discovered that he can look at a story without technically "watching" it, by tapping very quickly and not reading anything, which is a bit like claiming you did not eat the cake because you were chewing fast.

## Standard deviations

Under the Accords, the following are not violations:

- Looking at a *mutual friend's* story that happens to include a former realm
- Seeing a profile photo change through no fault of one's own
- Noticing, entirely by accident, that a certain realm has a new haircut

All three have occurred this week.

## The ruling

The Accords are suspended pending a review that will not take place. In the meantime the Signatory has been advised to touch grass, which he has acknowledged, noted, and filed under things to do after this next story.
