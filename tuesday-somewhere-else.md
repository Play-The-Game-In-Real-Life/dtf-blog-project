Nothing happens on this Tuesday.

That is what I want to tell you about. There is no trip, no fight, no anniversary, no grand gesture. We wake up a little late. The coffee is the wrong amount of strong. You find a sock under the bed that belongs to neither of us and we agree to say nothing about it.

At lunch you read me a sentence from the news, and I pretend I have not already read it. In the afternoon we work in the same room, not talking, with the particular quiet of two people who have stopped needing to. Around five, one of us says *what do you want for dinner*, and the other says *I don't know*, and that is the whole conversation, and it is perfect.

The evening goes the way evenings go. A show we have seen. A blanket that is not big enough, so we share it badly. You fall asleep first. I stay awake a little longer, just to notice.

This is the version I think about at night. I used to believe it was the big things I missed. It turns out it was the Tuesdays.
