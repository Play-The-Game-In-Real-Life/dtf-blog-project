dtf stands for **Damn These Feelings**. That is the whole name, and yes, I know what else those letters can mean. This is not that kind of website. This is the kind of website that processes its emotions by deploying a static site to a Cloudflare Worker.

## The premise

Every relationship I have had is a kingdom. Each one had a founding, a golden age, a few treaties I broke, and a collapse I am still trying to describe accurately. Instead of calling anyone about it, I decided to write the history down and publish it with an RSS feed.

That is a healthy coping mechanism. I looked it up.

## How the site works

Every ex gets a **realm**: a codename, a color, a dossier, and a page. Every chronicle I write is filed under a realm, so you can read one kingdom's whole story in order, or wander the whole map. The last realm is called the Capital, and it is my own head. It is under constant renovation and the walls are not load-bearing.

## About the narrator

I am the historian, and I am also in every single war. That is called a conflict of interest, and I am telling you up front so you can adjust for it. If a chronicle makes me sound reasonable, assume I wrote it that way on purpose.

## What you can expect

Lore, mostly. A fair amount of mockery, aimed at me. The occasional moment where the joke stops and something true gets through, because that is apparently how feelings work. They show up uninvited and ruin the bit.

Welcome to the realms.
