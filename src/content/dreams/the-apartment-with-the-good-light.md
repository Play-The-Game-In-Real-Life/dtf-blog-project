In this one, we found the apartment on the first try.

It was small, third floor, a little crooked in the way old buildings are. But the light came in through the south window at four in the afternoon and lay across the floor like something spilled on purpose. You stood in it and said *this one*, and we did not argue, because there was nothing to argue about.

We never bought a real table. We ate on the floor for most of a year, and called it a phase, and it was the best thing we ever did.

There was a plant that did not die. I would like that on the record, because in the real version, I am known for this. In the other version, I watered it, and it grew toward the window, and toward us, and eventually it had to be moved.

I think about that apartment more than I think about most places I have actually lived. I know where the bad outlet was. I know which stair was loud. I know the sound the radiator made on the first cold night, a sort of polite knock, like it wanted to be let in.

It never existed, and I still miss it.
