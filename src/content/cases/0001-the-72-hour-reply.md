*Recording begins. Interview room, no windows. A single mug of cold coffee is on the table.*

**Det. Holloway:** State your name for the record.

**Joe:** Joe. And before we start, I want to say the message was a lot.

**Det. Holloway:** It said "hey, are you free Thursday?"

**Joe:** It was a lot *emotionally*.

> **Exhibit A:** Read receipt. Time elapsed between delivery and read: three seconds.

**Det. Holloway:** Three seconds. You read it, you understood it, and you didn't respond.

**Joe:** I drafted a response. It was four paragraphs. It acknowledged the subtext.

**Det. Holloway:** There was no subtext. It was Thursday.

**Joe:** There's always subtext, Detective.

*Detective slides a second sheet across the table.*

**Det. Holloway:** On day three you sent fourteen messages. Fourteen. One of them is just the word "also."

**Joe:** I had a follow-up thought.

**Det. Holloway:** It was "also."

---

**Findings of the court:** The defendant is guilty on all counts, with the strong recommendation that he send the first draft, which is almost always fine.

**Sentence:** One reply, within the hour, to the next message he receives. Time served begins now.
