*Recording begins. A single drawer sits on the evidence table, labeled "Exhibit B."*

**Det. Holloway:** Walk me through the charger.

**Joe:** It was the good one. Eight feet. Never frayed. I let it be borrowed.

**Det. Holloway:** Borrowed.

**Joe:** Borrowed, in the sense that I assumed I'd get it back.

**Det. Holloway:** And you've held this grudge for how long?

**Joe:** A while.

*Detective opens the drawer. Inside are three chargers, none of them the right one.*

**Det. Holloway:** Is this one yours?

**Joe:** ...That one's hers.

**Det. Holloway:** And this one?

**Joe:** Also hers.

> **Exhibit B (inventory):** One (1) USB-C charger, white, frayed. One (1) lightning cable, other party's. One (1) mystery brick, unclaimed.

**Det. Holloway:** So you're holding someone else's chargers while holding a grudge about yours.

**Joe:** When you say it like that.

---

**Findings of the court:** The charger problem is mutual. Sentencing is pending, because the court has been unable to locate the original charger and has no reason to think it will be found.

**Recommendation:** Both parties buy a new charger and call it even.
